The idea

Four doors. Four tenants. One footprint.

Most storage gives you one big box and one key. A My Yard Vault 4 Corners multi-compartment building gives you four separate rooms, each behind its own reinforced, lockable door, each cross-ventilated, each ready to rent or assign. That's four streams of income, or four organized bays, from the same patch of ground.

  • Four separate rooms, four individual locks, true tenant separation
  • 265 cu ft per compartment, room for gear, stock, or a small storage unit
  • Cross-ventilation in every bay keeps contents dry
  • Reinforced floor and doors, built to be rented, not babied
  • Ships flat and relocates when your needs change
