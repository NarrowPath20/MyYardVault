Cookie policy

Updated October 7, 2026. This application does not set HTTP cookies or enable analytics, advertising pixels, or cross-site tracking.

Browser storage we use

  • yard-vault-theme: local storage remembers the theme you explicitly select until you change it or clear browser storage.
  • yard-vault-privacy: local storage remembers your acknowledgement of this notice if you select “Remember this choice.” The session-storage version only dismisses the notice for the current tab session when you select “Continue without saving.” Neither entry contains contact information.

Your choices

Both banner choices leave analytics and advertising disabled. There is no “accept all” option because no optional trackers are configured. You can browse and submit an inquiry without saving the notice.

Clear this site's browser storage in your browser settings to reset the theme and saved acknowledgement. Blocking storage does not prevent browsing. Third-party websites you choose to open can have their own storage and cookie practices.
