The one-trip difference

Used once. Not used up.

A “one-trip” container isn’t beat-up surplus. It’s manufactured to ISO spec, loaded with a single shipment for its maiden voyage to the States, and sold the moment it lands. That means factory-fresh steel, clean interiors, and doors that seal tight — without the price of building from scratch or the dents and rust of a decade at sea.

  • Near-new inside and out — clean walls, solid marine-plywood floor
  • 14-gauge Corten steel, wind- and water-tight
  • Double cargo doors with lock-box-ready hardware
  • CSC-plated and cargo-worthy, right off the truck
  • Ground-level access — no ramp and no lift needed
