Privacy policy

Updated October 7, 2026. This policy covers the My Yard Vault 4 Corners website and requests sent through its forms or Vault Assistant.

Who to contact

Contact My Yard Vault 4 Corners at info@myyardvault.com or 505-879-5331. See business details.

Information we collect

When you request a quote, visit, or financing information, we collect your name and at least one contact method. You may also provide a city or ZIP code, product preferences, a preferred visit time, and a message. We record your request reference, submission time, page of origin, and your contact and adult confirmations. Please do not submit identity documents, payment card details, Social Security numbers, health information, or information about children.

Chat answers run in your browser. The conversation is not sent to an AI provider or saved as a transcript by this application. Contact details are sent to our server only when you confirm and send a request. Hosting providers may separately keep access and security logs.

How information is used and shared

We use inquiry information to respond to your request and coordinate the service you asked about. Submitting a request does not enroll you in marketing. This site does not run advertising or analytics trackers and does not sell inquiry information. If internal SMS alerts are enabled, Twilio receives the request type and reference for an alert to our sales team, without your name, contact details, location, or message; this integration does not text customers. Hosting and communications providers may process information to provide these services. We may also disclose information when legally required.

Browser storage and third parties

Theme and privacy choices use browser storage as explained in the cookie policy. Fonts and the product viewer are served from this website. Links you choose to visit on another website are governed by that site's policies.

Retention and security

New inquiries submitted under this policy are removed from the website's inquiry store after 180 days during routine cleanup. Earlier inquiries, accepted orders, legally required records, business correspondence, provider records, and backups require separate retention review. We restrict access to saved inquiries; no online system can guarantee absolute security. Ask us about the specific records associated with your request.

Your choices

You can request access, correction, deletion, or a stop to optional follow-up. Use our data deletion instructions or communication preferences. We may verify your request using the contact information already supplied. We explain any records we must retain. Available legal rights depend on where you live.

Children

These inquiry forms are intended for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information. Do not send a child's details. If you believe a child submitted information, contact us so we can review and remove it. An adult confirmation is not parental consent for collecting children's information.

Changes

We update the date above when this policy changes. Contact us if you need clarification before submitting information.
