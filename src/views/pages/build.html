What shows up

The whole vault, on one pallet.

Every Yard Vault ships as a single flat, shrink-wrapped stack — nothing loose, nothing missing. Unwrap it and everything you need is right there:

  • Galvanized steel floor pan
  • Corrugated 20-gauge wall panels
  • Roof panels & ridge cap
  • Lockable steel door(s)
  • Gas-strut & latch hardware
  • Every bolt, plus an assembly guide
