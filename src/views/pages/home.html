Questions, answered

Everything people ask before they buy.

Will it really fit through my gate?
Yes — that’s the whole point of the design. Every My Yard Vault 4 Corners ships as flat panels that roll through a standard yard gate, then bolt together on your land. No crane, no fence coming down, no torn-up driveway.
How long does the build take?
About an hour for most sizes. Our crew assembles the galvanized panels on-site, sets the doors and locks, and walks you through it before we leave.
Do I need a concrete slab?
No. My Yard Vault 4 Corners units sit on leveled blocks on firm ground — the same way you’ve seen them in our install photos. If you already have a slab or pad, we’ll happily build on that too.
Do I need a permit?
In most Four Corners jurisdictions, small detached structures like these don’t require a permit — but rules vary by city, county, and HOA. We recommend a quick check with your local office, and we can point you to what to ask.
Will it rust?
The panels are 20-gauge galvanized steel — the zinc coating is what protects highway guardrails from the same sun, snow, and monsoon seasons. No rot, no termites, no warping, and no annual repainting.
How secure is it?
Every unit closes on recessed, lockable T-handle latches with room for your own padlock, and the panel system leaves no exposed hinge pins to pull. See the accessories page for the locking hardware up close.
Can I move it later?
Yes. The same bolts that put it up take it down. If you move house or want it on the other side of the yard, it unbolts, travels flat, and goes back up.
How do payments work?
Two partner options: financing and credit through Synchrony, or lease-to-own with no credit needed through Acima. Try the payment estimator to ballpark a monthly number, then apply in minutes.
